## Identity lock

- **少爷**: fluffy longhair, white chest/face, brown-grey tabby on head/back, bushy dark-tipped tail, green-gold eyes, pink nose; larger.
- **可丽**: golden British Shorthair, round face, cream chest/muzzle, large green eyes, pink nose, thick dark-tipped tail; smaller/rounder.
- Style: soft realistic 3D. No Q-version, no flat illustration, no clothes unless asked.
